# ProjectX — Attribution & Lineage

This unified application fuses and adapts the best capabilities of two reference repositories developed for the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026)**, Problem Statement ID **26250** (*Air Power - Dynamic Air Operations & Resource Optimisation*, Ministry of Defence / Defence Services Staff College):

1. **AERIS**
   - **Repository**: [https://github.com/sahilgaurav18/AERIS.git](https://github.com/sahilgaurav18/AERIS.git)
   - **Author**: Sahil Gaurav & Team
   - **Core Features Adapted**:
     - Trained Machine Learning models (`RandomForestRegressor` and `Classifier` for aircraft degradation, multi-horizon fleet readiness forecasting, and mission operational risk).
     - Causal Chain Contingency Analysis (*What Changed → What Was Affected → Why → Resulting Impact*).
     - Multi-posture optimization balancing (Balanced, Reserve-Focused, High-Tempo).
     - Airspace tactical visualization concepts & C2 Copilot Tactical Advisory reasoning.

2. **VYUHA (SIH-PS-26250)**
   - **Repository**: [https://github.com/HAWKAARJAV/SIH-PS-26250.git](https://github.com/HAWKAARJAV/SIH-PS-26250.git)
   - **Author**: Hawkaarjav & Team
   - **Core Features Adapted**:
     - Relational domain schema (Bases, Aircraft, Crew, Stores/ATF/Munitions, Missions, ATO Plans, Events).
     - Cryptographic SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail and tamper-verification system.
     - 8-Feed Telemetry Fusion engine with status degradation/restoration controls and Conflict Inbox.
     - Google OR-Tools CP-SAT tasking engine & Independent Constraint Validator.
     - Role-Based Access Control (RBAC: Commander, Ops Planner, Auditor, Fleet Officer, Crew Officer, Analyst, Admin).
     - Dynamic Retasking & Disruption Injection with server-ranked Courses of Action (COAs).

Synthesized and engineered into **PROJECTX** as a unified, production-grade air combat operations command and decision-support platform.
